SAT Math Reference Information

Geometriya va Doira formulalari:

$A = \pi r^2$, $C = 2\pi r$, $A = l \cdot w$, $A = \frac{1}{2} b \cdot h$

Pifagor teoremasi: $c^2 = a^2 + b^2$

Hajm formulalari ($V$):

To'g'ri burchakli parallelepiped: $V = l \cdot w \cdot h$

Silindr: $V = \pi r^2 h$

Shar (Sfera): $V = \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3$

Konus: $V = \frac{1}{3} \pi r^2 h$

Piramida: $V = \frac{1}{3} B h$

Maxsus uchburchaklar:

$30^\circ - 60^\circ - 90^\circ$: Tomonlar nisbati $x : x\sqrt{3} : 2x$

$45^\circ - 45^\circ - 90^\circ$: Tomonlar nisbati $x : x : x\sqrt{2}$

Doiradagi burchaklar yig'indisi: $360^\circ$ ($2\pi$ radian). Uchburchak ichki burchaklari yig'indisi: $180^\circ$.
